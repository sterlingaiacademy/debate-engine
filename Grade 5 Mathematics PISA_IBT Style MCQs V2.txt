Grade 5 Mathematics Challenge: Everyday Math Explorers 
Welcome to your math adventure! Each question below includes the correct answer and a step-by-step explanation to help you learn.
Section 1: Number & Operations in Daily Life

1. The School Library Books
A primary school library receives 4 boxes of new books. Each box contains 25 books. How many new books does the library receive in total?
A) 80 books
B) 100 books ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 120 books
D) 125 books
Explanation: Multiply the number of boxes by the number of books in each box: 4 × 25 = 100 total books.

2. Baking Cupcakes
Sarah bakes 48 delicious chocolate cupcakes for a class party. She wants to share them equally among 6 tables. How many cupcakes will each table get?
A) 6 cupcakes
B) 7 cupcakes
C) 8 cupcakes ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 9 cupcakes
Explanation: Divide the total number of cupcakes by the number of tables: 48 ÷ 6 = 8 cupcakes per table.

3. Saving for a Toy Car
Liam saves $15 every week from his pocket money. How much money will he have saved after 6 weeks?
A) $75
B) $80
C) $90 ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) $100
Explanation: Multiply the weekly savings by the number of weeks: $15 × 6 = $90.

4. Fruit Basket Fractions
In a fruit bowl, there are 10 fruits in total: 3 apples, 4 bananas, and 3 oranges. What fraction of the fruit bowl is made up of bananas?
A) 3/10
B) 4/10 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 6/10
D) 7/10
Explanation: The fraction of bananas is the number of bananas over the total number of fruits: 4 out of 10, which is 4/10.

5. Decimal Juice Bottles
Emma buys two bottles of juice. One bottle contains 1.25 liters and the other contains 0.75 liters. How many liters of juice does she have altogether?
A) 1.50 liters
B) 1.75 liters
C) 2.00 liters ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 2.25 liters
Explanation: Add the two decimal amounts together: 1.25 + 0.75 = 2.00 liters.

6. Pizza Party Fractions
A pizza is divided into 8 equal slices. Alex eats 2 slices and Maya eats 3 slices. What fraction of the pizza is left over?
A) 3/8 ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) 4/8
C) 5/8
D) 6/8
Explanation: Total slices eaten = 2 + 3 = 5 slices (5/8). Remaining slices = 8 - 5 = 3 slices, which is 3/8 of the pizza.

7. Grocery Store Rounding
A bag of apples costs $4.87. What is $4.87 rounded to the nearest dollar?
A) $4.00
B) $4.50
C) $4.90
D) $5.00 ✔ (Correct Answer)
Explanation: Look at the tenths place (.8). Since 8 is 5 or greater, round up to the next whole dollar, which is $5.00.
Section 2: Patterns, Logic & Sequences

8. The Bus Schedule Pattern
Buses leave the station at regular intervals: 8:05 AM, 8:20 AM, 8:35 AM, and 8:50 AM. Following this pattern, when will the next bus leave?
A) 9:00 AM
B) 9:05 AM ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 9:10 AM
D) 9:15 AM
Explanation: The time increases by 15 minutes each step. Adding 15 minutes to 8:50 AM gives 9:05 AM.

9. Tile Pattern Challenge
A decorative floor design follows a growing tile pattern:
Step 1: 3 tiles | Step 2: 6 tiles | Step 3: 9 tiles | Step 4: 12 tiles
How many tiles will be needed for Step 6?
A) 15 tiles
B) 18 tiles ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 21 tiles
D) 24 tiles
Explanation: The pattern increases by 3 tiles per step (Step number × 3). For Step 6: 6 × 3 = 18 tiles.

10. Missing Number Mystery
Look at this number sequence: 5, 10, 20, 40, ___, 160. What is the missing number?
A) 50
B) 60
C) 80 ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 100
Explanation: Each number is doubled (multiplied by 2) to get the next number. 40 × 2 = 80 (and 80 × 2 = 160).

11. Number Machine Rule
An input-output machine transforms numbers:
Input 2 → Output 7
Input 4 → Output 11
Input 6 → Output 15
If the input is 10, what will be the output?
A) 19
B) 21
C) 23 ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 25
Explanation: The rule is (Input × 2) + 3. For an input of 10: (10 × 2) + 3 = 20 + 3 = 23.

12. House Numbers on Green Street
All houses on the north side of Green Street have even numbers: 102, 104, 106, 108... What will be the number of the 7th house in this row?
A) 110
B) 112
C) 114 ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 116
Explanation: Counting consecutive even numbers starting from 102: 102 (1st), 104 (2nd), 106 (3rd), 108 (4th), 110 (5th), 112 (6th), 114 (7th).
Section 3: Geometry & Spatial Awareness

13. Fencing the Garden
Mr. Green is building a rectangular vegetable garden that is 8 meters long and 5 meters wide. How many meters of fencing does he need to put all around the border?
A) 13 meters
B) 26 meters ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 40 meters
D) 50 meters
Explanation: Perimeter = 2 × (Length + Width) = 2 × (8 + 5) = 2 × 13 = 26 meters.

14. Carpet Area
A square rug measures 4 meters on each side. What is the total area covered by the rug in square meters?
A) 8 sq m
B) 12 sq m
C) 16 sq m ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 20 sq m
Explanation: Area of a square = side × side = 4 × 4 = 16 square meters.

15. Identifying Triangles
A triangle has three equal sides of length 6 cm each. What type of triangle is this?
A) Right-angled triangle
B) Isosceles triangle
C) Equilateral triangle ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) Scalene triangle
Explanation: A triangle with all three equal sides is called an equilateral triangle.

16. Clock Hands Angle
When a clock displays exactly 3:00, what kind of angle is formed between the hour hand and the minute hand?
A) Acute angle
B) Right angle ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) Obtuse angle
D) Straight angle
Explanation: At 3:00, the minute hand points at 12 and the hour hand points at 3, forming a 90-degree right angle.

17. Symmetry in Shapes
How many lines of symmetry does a standard regular square have?
A) 1
B) 2
C) 4 ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 8
Explanation: A square has 4 lines of symmetry: 1 vertical, 1 horizontal, and 2 diagonal lines.

18. Gift Box Nets
Which 3D shape can be constructed by folding six identical square faces together?
A) Pyramid
B) Cylinder
C) Rectangular Prism
D) Cube ✔ (Correct Answer)
Explanation: A cube is a 3D shape composed of 6 identical square faces.
Section 4: Measurement, Time & Data Analysis

19. Morning Bus Ride
Noah gets on the school bus at 7:45 AM. The ride takes 35 minutes. At what time does he arrive at school?
A) 8:15 AM
B) 8:20 AM ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 8:25 AM
D) 8:30 AM
Explanation: Adding 15 minutes to 7:45 AM brings it to 8:00 AM. Adding the remaining 20 minutes gives 8:20 AM.

20. Baking Conversion
A cake recipe requires 2.5 kilograms of flour. How many grams is this equal to?
A) 250 grams
B) 2,500 grams ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 25,000 grams
D) 250,000 grams
Explanation: Since 1 kilogram = 1,000 grams, multiply 2.5 × 1,000 = 2,500 grams.

21. Water Jug Measurement
A water jug holds 3 liters of water. If David drinks 750 milliliters from the jug, how much water is left inside?
A) 2,250 milliliters ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) 2,500 milliliters
C) 2,750 milliliters
D) 3,250 milliliters
Explanation: Convert liters to milliliters: 3 liters = 3,000 ml. Subtract the amount drunk: 3,000 ml - 750 ml = 2,250 ml.

22. Favorite Pets Survey
In a survey of 30 Grade 5 students:
• 12 chose Dogs
• 10 chose Cats
• 5 chose Fish
• The remaining students chose Birds.
How many students chose Birds as their favorite pet?
A) 2 students
B) 3 students ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 4 students
D) 5 students
Explanation: Sum of students accounted for = 12 + 10 + 5 = 27. Remaining students who chose Birds = 30 - 27 = 3 students.

23. Reading Temperature
At 6:00 AM, the outside temperature was 18°C. By 1:00 PM, the temperature rose by 9°C. What was the temperature at 1:00 PM?
A) 25°C
B) 27°C ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 28°C
D) 29°C
Explanation: Add the temperature rise to the initial temperature: 18°C + 9°C = 27°C.

24. Weekly Test Scores Average
Mia scores 80, 90, and 100 on three weekly math quizzes. What is her average (mean) quiz score?
A) 85
B) 90 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 95
D) 100
Explanation: Calculate the sum and divide by total quizzes: (80 + 90 + 100) ÷ 3 = 270 ÷ 3 = 90.

25. Probability with Colored Marbles
A pouch contains 5 red marbles, 5 blue marbles, and 10 green marbles. If you reach in without looking, which color are you most likely to pull out?
A) Red
B) Blue
C) Green ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) Red and Blue are equally likely
Explanation: Green has the highest quantity (10 out of 20 total marbles), giving it the highest probability of being picked.
