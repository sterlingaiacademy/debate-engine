Section 1: Real Number Systems & Algebraic Polynomials

1. Laws of Rational Exponents
Simplify the expression (16x4)1/2 for x > 0.
A) 4x
B) 4x2 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 8x2
D) 16x2
Explanation: Apply the exponent 1/2 to both the coefficient and term: 161/2 · (x4)1/2 = √16 · x(4 · 1/2) = 4x2.

2. Adding Polynomials in Real-World Context
A company's total revenue is modeled by R(x) = 3x2 + 20x + 100, and its operating costs are C(x) = x2 + 8x + 40, where x is units sold. What polynomial represents the net profit P(x) = R(x) - C(x)?
A) 2x2 + 12x + 60 ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) 4x2 + 28x + 140
C) 2x2 + 28x + 60
D) 3x2 + 12x + 60
Explanation: Subtract corresponding terms: (3x2 - x2) + (20x - 8x) + (100 - 40) = 2x2 + 12x + 60.

3. Factoring Trinomials
Factor the quadratic expression completely: x2 - 7x + 12.
A) (x + 3)(x + 4)
B) (x - 3)(x - 4) ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) (x - 2)(x - 6)
D) (x + 2)(x - 6)
Explanation: Find two numbers that multiply to +12 and add up to -7. These numbers are -3 and -4. Thus, (x - 3)(x - 4).

4. Difference of Squares
Which expression is equivalent to 9x2 - 25?
A) (3x - 5)2
B) (3x - 5)(3x + 5) ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) (9x - 5)(x + 5)
D) (3x + 5)2
Explanation: Using the difference of squares formula a2 - b2 = (a - b)(a + b) with a = 3x and b = 5 yields (3x - 5)(3x + 5).

5. Multiplying Binomials
Expand the expression: (2x - 3)(x + 5).
A) 2x2 + 10x - 15
B) 2x2 + 7x - 15 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 2x2 - 7x - 15
D) 2x2 + 13x - 15
Explanation: Using FOIL: First = 2x2, Outer = 10x, Inner = -3x, Last = -15. Combining terms: 2x2 + 7x - 15.

6. Rational Expressions Simplification
Simplify (x2 - 9) / (x - 3) for x ≠ 3.
A) x + 3 ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) x - 3
C) x + 9
D) 1
Explanation: Factor numerator: (x - 3)(x + 3) / (x - 3). Cancel common factor (x - 3) to get x + 3.
Section 2: Linear & Quadratic Functions and Relations

7. Finding X- and Y-Intercepts
What are the intercepts of the linear equation 3x + 4y = 12?
A) x-intercept = (4, 0), y-intercept = (0, 3) ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) x-intercept = (3, 0), y-intercept = (0, 4)
C) x-intercept = (-4, 0), y-intercept = (0, -3)
D) x-intercept = (12, 0), y-intercept = (0, 12)
Explanation: Set y = 0 for x-intercept: 3x = 12 → x = 4 (4, 0). Set x = 0 for y-intercept: 4y = 12 → y = 3 (0, 3).

8. Parallel and Perpendicular Line Slopes
Line A has an equation of y = -2x + 5. What is the slope of a line that is perpendicular to Line A?
A) m = -2
B) m = 2
C) m = 1/2 ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) m = -1/2
Explanation: Perpendicular lines have negative reciprocal slopes. The negative reciprocal of -2 is +1/2.

9. Vertex Form of a Parabola
A quadratic function is given by f(x) = (x - 3)2 + 4. What are the coordinates of the vertex of the parabola?
A) (-3, 4)
B) (3, 4) ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) (3, -4)
D) (-3, -4)
Explanation: Vertex form is f(x) = a(x - h)2 + k with vertex (h, k). Here h = 3 and k = 4, so the vertex is (3, 4).

10. Solving Quadratic Equations by Factoring
What are the roots of the equation x2 - 5x - 14 = 0?
A) x = 5 and x = -14
B) x = 7 and x = -2 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) x = -7 and x = 2
D) x = 7 and x = 2
Explanation: Factor: (x - 7)(x + 2) = 0. Set each factor to zero: x - 7 = 0 → x = 7; x + 2 = 0 → x = -2.

11. Using the Discriminant
How many real solutions does the quadratic equation 2x2 - 4x + 5 = 0 have?
A) No real solutions ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) Exactly one real solution
C) Two real solutions
D) Infinitely many real solutions
Explanation: Discriminant Δ = b2 - 4ac = (-4)2 - 4(2)(5) = 16 - 40 = -24. Since Δ < 0, there are no real solutions.

12. Domain and Range
What is the domain of the function f(x) = √(x - 4)?
A) All real numbers
B) x > 4
C) x ≥ 4 ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) x ≤ 4
Explanation: The expression under a square root must be non-negative: x - 4 ≥ 0 → x ≥ 4.

13. Compound Inequalities
Solve the inequality: -3 < 2x + 1 ≤ 7.
A) -2 < x ≤ 3 ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) -1 < x ≤ 4
C) -2 ≤ x < 3
D) -4 < x ≤ 6
Explanation: Subtract 1 from all parts: -4 < 2x ≤ 6. Divide all parts by 2: -2 < x ≤ 3.
Section 3: Advanced Geometry & Trigonometry

14. Right Triangle Trigonometry (Sine)
In a right-angled triangle, the angle θ has an opposite side of length 6 cm and a hypotenuse of length 10 cm. What is sin(θ)?
A) 0.6 ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) 0.8
C) 0.75
D) 1.33
Explanation: sin(θ) = Opposite / Hypotenuse = 6 / 10 = 0.6.

15. Right Triangle Trigonometry (Tangent)
A flagpole casts a shadow of 12 meters when the sun's angle of elevation is 45°. How tall is the flagpole?
A) 6 meters
B) 12 meters ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 12√2 meters
D) 24 meters
Explanation: tan(45°) = Height / Shadow → 1 = Height / 12 → Height = 12 meters.

16. Similar Triangles Scale Factor
Triangle ABC is similar to Triangle DEF. If AB = 8 cm, DE = 12 cm, and BC = 10 cm, what is the length of EF?
A) 12 cm
B) 15 cm ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 16 cm
D) 20 cm
Explanation: Ratio of corresponding sides = DE / AB = 12 / 8 = 1.5. Length EF = BC · 1.5 = 10 · 1.5 = 15 cm.

17. Inscribed Angle Theorem
An angle inscribed in a circle subtends an arc measuring 80°. What is the measure of the inscribed angle?
A) 40° ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) 80°
C) 120°
D) 160°
Explanation: An inscribed angle is equal to half the measure of its intercepted arc: 80° / 2 = 40°.

18. Surface Area of a Cylinder
A cylindrical can has a radius of 3 cm and a height of 7 cm. What is its total surface area including top and bottom caps? (Use π ≈ 22/7)
A) 132 cm2
B) 188.57 cm2 (188 4/7) ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 210 cm2
D) 264 cm2
Explanation: Surface Area = 2πr(r + h) = 2 · (22/7) · 3 · (3 + 7) = (132/7) · 10 = 1320/7 ≈ 188.57 cm2.

19. Coordinate Geometry Quadrants & Midpoints
What is the midpoint of the line segment joining points A(-4, 6) and B(2, -2)?
A) (-1, 2) ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) (-2, 4)
C) (1, -2)
D) (-3, 2)
Explanation: Midpoint formula = ((x1 + x2)/2, (y1 + y2)/2) = ((-4 + 2)/2, (6 + -2)/2) = (-2/2, 4/2) = (-1, 2).
Section 4: Statistics, Probability & Logical Reasoning

20. Standard Deviation Concept
Two classes take the same test. Both classes achieve a mean score of 75. Class A has a standard deviation of 4, while Class B has a standard deviation of 12. What does this indicate?
A) Class B scored higher overall.
B) Class B scores are much more spread out around the mean. ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) Class A has a wider spread of test performance.
D) Students in Class A made more errors.
Explanation: Standard deviation measures dispersion; a larger value means scores are more spread out from the mean.

21. Box Plot Interpretation
A box plot shows Q1 = 25, Median = 35, and Q3 = 50. What is the Interquartile Range (IQR)?
A) 10
B) 25 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 35
D) 60
Explanation: Interquartile Range IQR = Q3 - Q1 = 50 - 25 = 25.

22. Conditional Probability
Out of 100 students, 60 study French, 40 study Spanish, and 20 study both. If a student selected at random studies French, what is the probability they also study Spanish?
A) 1/5
B) 1/3 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 1/2
D) 2/5
Explanation: P(Spanish | French) = P(Both) / P(French) = 20 / 60 = 1/3.

23. Exponential Growth Model
A town's population of 10,000 grows at a rate of 5% per year. Which equation models the population P after t years?
A) P = 10,000(1.05)t ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) P = 10,000(0.05)t
C) P = 10,000 + 1.05t
D) P = 10,000(0.95)t
Explanation: Exponential growth formula P = P0(1 + r)t = 10,000(1 + 0.05)t = 10,000(1.05)t.

24. Arithmetic Sequences
What is the 10th term of the arithmetic sequence: 4, 9, 14, 19, ...?
A) 44
B) 49 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 50
D) 54
Explanation: First term a1 = 4, common difference d = 5. Formula: an = a1 + (n - 1)d → a10 = 4 + (9)(5) = 4 + 45 = 49.

25. Geometric Sequences
What is the common ratio of the geometric sequence: 81, 27, 9, 3, ...?
A) 1/3 ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) 3
C) -3
D) -1/3
Explanation: Common ratio r = termn / termn-1 = 27 / 81 = 1/3.
