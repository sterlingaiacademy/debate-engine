Grade 8 Mathematics Challenge: Real-World Math Explorers
Welcome to the Grade 8 Math Challenge! Read each scenario carefully, select the best option, and review the detailed step-by-step explanation below each question.
Section 1: The Number System & Exponents

1. Distance in Scientific Notation
The average distance from the Earth to the Sun is approximately 150,000,000 kilometers. How is this distance written in proper scientific notation?
A) 15 × 107 km
B) 1.5 × 108 km ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 1.5 × 107 km
D) 0.15 × 109 km
Explanation: Scientific notation requires a coefficient between 1 and 10 multiplied by a power of 10. Moving the decimal point 8 places to the left gives 1.5 × 108.

2. Microscopic Measurements
The width of a cell structure is 0.0000045 meters. How is this number represented in scientific notation?
A) 4.5 × 10-6 m ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) 4.5 × 10-5 m
C) 45 × 10-7 m
D) 4.5 × 106 m
Explanation: Move the decimal point 6 places to the right to obtain a number between 1 and 10 (4.5). Moving to the right gives a negative exponent: 4.5 × 10-6.

3. Simplifying Exponent Rules
What is the simplified value of (32 × 34) / 33?
A) 3
B) 9
C) 27 ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 81
Explanation: Using exponent rules: 3(2+4) / 33 = 36 / 33 = 3(6-3) = 33 = 27.

4. Square Roots in Real Life
A square community garden has an area of 144 square meters. What is the length of one side of the garden?
A) 11 meters
B) 12 meters ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 14 meters
D) 36 meters
Explanation: The side length of a square is the square root of its area: √144 = 12 meters (since 12 × 12 = 144).

5. Approximating Irrational Numbers
Between which two consecutive whole numbers does √30 lie?
A) 4 and 5
B) 5 and 6 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 6 and 7
D) 15 and 16
Explanation: Identify perfect squares surrounding 30: 25 < 30 < 36. Taking square roots gives √25 < √30 < √36, so 5 < √30 < 6.

6. Rational vs. Irrational Numbers
Which of the following values represents an irrational number?
A) √49
B) 0.333...
C) 5/8
D) √10 ✔ (Correct Answer)
Explanation: √49 = 7 (rational), 0.333... = 1/3 (rational), and 5/8 is a ratio of integers (rational). √10 cannot be written as a simple fraction and has a non-repeating, non-terminating decimal, making it irrational.

7. Zero and Negative Exponents
What is the value of 5-2 + 40?
A) 1/25
B) 1 1/25 (1.04) ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) -25
D) 0
Explanation: 5-2 = 1 / (52) = 1/25. Any non-zero number to the power of 0 equals 1 (40 = 1). Thus, 1/25 + 1 = 1.04.
Section 2: Expressions, Equations & Linear Functions

8. Solving Multi-Step Equations
Solve for x: 4(x - 3) = 2x + 10.
A) x = 5
B) x = 11 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) x = 12
D) x = 22
Explanation: Expand left side: 4x - 12 = 2x + 10. Subtract 2x from both sides: 2x - 12 = 10. Add 12 to both sides: 2x = 22. Divide by 2: x = 11.

9. Finding the Slope of a Line
A hiking path rises 15 meters vertically for every 30 meters of horizontal distance. What is the slope (m) of the path?
A) m = 1/3
B) m = 1/2 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) m = 2
D) m = 3
Explanation: Slope = Rise / Run = 15 / 30 = 1/2.

10. Identifying Slope-Intercept Form
A cell phone plan costs a flat fee of $20 per month plus $0.10 per text message sent. Which equation represents the monthly cost (y) for sending x text messages?
A) y = 0.10x + 20 ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) y = 20x + 0.10
C) y = 20.10x
D) y = 0.10x - 20
Explanation: In slope-intercept form y = mx + b, m is the rate of change ($0.10 per text) and b is the initial fixed cost ($20). Thus, y = 0.10x + 20.

11. Number of Solutions to an Equation
How many solutions does the equation 3x + 5 = 3x + 9 have?
A) No solution ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) Exactly one solution
C) Exactly two solutions
D) Infinitely many solutions
Explanation: Subtracting 3x from both sides yields 5 = 9, which is a false statement. Therefore, there is no solution.

12. Systems of Linear Equations by Substitution
Solve the system of equations:
y = 2x
x + y = 9
A) (2, 4)
B) (3, 6) ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) (6, 3)
D) (4, 5)
Explanation: Substitute y = 2x into the second equation: x + 2x = 9 → 3x = 9 → x = 3. Substitute x = 3 into y = 2x → y = 6. Solution is (3, 6).

13. Function Rules & Graphs
Which set of ordered pairs (x, y) represents a valid function?
A) {(1, 2), (1, 3), (2, 4)}
B) {(1, 5), (2, 5), (3, 6)} ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) {(3, 1), (3, 2), (3, 3)}
D) {(4, 0), (4, 1), (5, 2)}
Explanation: A function requires every input (x-value) to map to exactly one output (y-value). Option B has unique x-values {1, 2, 3}. Options A, C, and D repeat x-values with different y-values.
Section 3: Geometry & The Pythagorean Theorem

14. The Pythagorean Theorem in Construction
A builder leans an 10-meter ladder against a wall. The base of the ladder is placed 6 meters away from the wall. How high up the wall does the ladder reach?
A) 7 meters
B) 8 meters ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 9 meters
D) 12 meters
Explanation: Use a² + b² = c² where hypotenuse c = 10 m and base a = 6 m. 6² + b² = 10² → 36 + b² = 100 → b² = 64 → b = √64 = 8 meters.

15. Distance Between Coordinates
What is the straight-line distance between the points (1, 2) and (4, 6) on a coordinate plane?
A) 4 units
B) 5 units ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 6 units
D) 7 units
Explanation: Δx = 4 - 1 = 3; Δy = 6 - 2 = 4. Distance = √(Δx² + Δy²) = √(3² + 4²) = √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5 units.

16. Volume of a Cylinder
A cylindrical water tank has a radius of 3 meters and a height of 5 meters. What is its volume? (Use π ≈ 3.14)
A) 47.1 m³
B) 94.2 m³
C) 141.3 m³ ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 282.6 m³
Explanation: Volume = πr²h = 3.14 × 3² × 5 = 3.14 × 9 × 5 = 3.14 × 45 = 141.3 cubic meters.

17. Volume of a Cone
A party hat in the shape of a cone has a base radius of 6 cm and a height of 10 cm. What is its volume? (Use π ≈ 3.14)
A) 125.6 cm³
B) 188.4 cm³
C) 376.8 cm³ ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 1,130.4 cm³
Explanation: Volume = (1/3)πr²h = (1/3) × 3.14 × 6² × 10 = (1/3) × 3.14 × 36 × 10 = 3.14 × 12 × 10 = 376.8 cm³.

18. Geometric Transformations (Dilations)
A triangle with vertex coordinates A(2, 4) is dilated by a scale factor of 1.5 centered at the origin. What are the new coordinates of A'?
A) (3, 6) ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) (3.5, 5.5)
C) (4, 8)
D) (2.5, 4.5)
Explanation: Multiply each coordinate by the scale factor 1.5: A' = (2 × 1.5, 4 × 1.5) = (3, 6).

19. Parallel Lines Cut by a Transversal
Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal line. If one interior angle is 70°, what is the measure of its alternate interior angle?
A) 70° ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) 110°
C) 180°
D) 20°
Explanation: When parallel lines are intersected by a transversal, alternate interior angles are congruent (equal in measure). Thus, it is 70°.
Section 4: Data Analysis & Bivariate Data

20. Scatter Plot Trends
A teacher records study time (hours) and test scores (%) for students. The scatter plot shows that as study time increases, test scores generally increase. What type of association is this?
A) Positive association ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) Negative association
C) No association
D) Constant non-linear association
Explanation: When both variables increase together, the scatter plot displays a positive linear association.

21. Line of Best Fit Equation
The line of best fit for ice cream sales (y in dollars) based on daily temperature (x in °C) is given by y = 12x - 50. What is the predicted sales amount when the temperature is 25°C?
A) $200
B) $250 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) $300
D) $350
Explanation: Substitute x = 25 into the equation: y = 12(25) - 50 = 300 - 50 = $250.

22. Two-Way Frequency Tables
In a survey of 100 students:
• 60 students play a sport (40 boys, 20 girls)
• 40 students do not play a sport (10 boys, 30 girls)
How many total boys participated in the survey?
A) 40
B) 50 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 60
D) 70
Explanation: Sum the boys who play sports and boys who do not: 40 + 10 = 50 total boys.

23. Volume of a Sphere
A basketball has a radius of 12 cm. What is its volume? (Use π ≈ 3.14)
A) 1,808.64 cm³
B) 7,234.56 cm³ ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 5,425.92 cm³
D) 2,411.52 cm³
Explanation: Volume = (4/3)πr³ = (4/3) × 3.14 × 12³ = (4/3) × 3.14 × 1728 = 4 × 3.14 × 576 = 7,234.56 cubic cm.

24. Exterior Angle Theorem of Triangles
An exterior angle of a triangle measures 120°. If one of the remote interior angles measures 50°, what is the measure of the second remote interior angle?
A) 70° ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) 80°
C) 110°
D) 130°
Explanation: According to the Exterior Angle Theorem, an exterior angle equals the sum of the two remote interior angles: 120° = 50° + x → x = 120° - 50° = 70°.

25. Congruence via Rigid Transformations
Shape A is reflected across the y-axis and then translated 3 units down to form Shape B. Which statement correctly describes the relationship between Shape A and Shape B?
A) Shape B is larger than Shape A.
B) Shape B is similar but not congruent to Shape A.
C) Shape B is congruent to Shape A. ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) Shape B has a different area than Shape A.
Explanation: Reflections and translations are rigid transformations. They preserve angle measurements and side lengths, meaning the original and image shapes remain fully congruent.
