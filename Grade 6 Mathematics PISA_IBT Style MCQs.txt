Grade 6 Mathematics Challenge: Real-World Math Explorers
Welcome to Grade 6 Math Challenge! Read each scenario carefully, select the correct option, and review the explanations to deepen your mathematical reasoning.
Section 1: Ratios, Proportions & Percentages

1. School Lemonade Stand Ratio
For a school fundraiser, students mix 2 cups of lemon juice with 5 cups of water to make lemonade. If they use 6 cups of lemon juice, how many cups of water do they need to keep the same taste?
A) 10 cups
B) 15 cups ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 18 cups
D) 20 cups
Explanation: The ratio of lemon juice to water is 2 : 5. Multiplying the lemon juice by 3 (2 × 3 = 6) means we must also multiply the water by 3: 5 × 3 = 15 cups.

2. Discount on a Backpack
A school store offers a 20% discount on a backpack that originally costs $40. How much money do you save with the discount?
A) $4
B) $8 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) $10
D) $32
Explanation: Calculate 20% of $40: (20 / 100) × 40 = 0.20 × 40 = $8 savings.

3. Classroom Gender Ratio
In a Grade 6 class of 30 students, 18 are girls and 12 are boys. What is the simplified ratio of girls to total students?
A) 2 : 3
B) 3 : 5 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 3 : 2
D) 5 : 3
Explanation: Ratio of girls to total students = 18 : 30. Divide both numbers by their greatest common factor (6): 18 ÷ 6 = 3 and 30 ÷ 6 = 5, giving 3 : 5.

4. Unit Price Shopping
A supermarket sells a pack of 4 notebooks for $6.00 and a pack of 10 identical notebooks for $13.00. What is the cost per notebook in the 4-pack?
A) $1.25
B) $1.50 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) $1.60
D) $2.00
Explanation: Unit price = Total Cost ÷ Quantity = $6.00 ÷ 4 = $1.50 per notebook.

5. Test Score Percentage
Daniel scored 21 out of 25 on his science quiz. What percentage score did Daniel get?
A) 75%
B) 80%
C) 84% ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 88%
Explanation: Fraction = 21 / 25. Multiply numerator and denominator by 4 to get a fraction over 100: (21 × 4) / (25 × 4) = 84 / 100 = 84%.

6. Speed and Distance
A cyclist travels at a constant speed of 12 kilometers per hour. How many kilometers will the cyclist cover in 2.5 hours?
A) 24 km
B) 30 km ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 36 km
D) 48 km
Explanation: Distance = Speed × Time = 12 km/h × 2.5 hours = 30 km.

7. Model Scale Conversion
On a map, 1 centimeter represents an actual distance of 5 kilometers. If two towns are 7 centimeters apart on the map, what is the actual distance between them?
A) 25 km
B) 30 km
C) 35 km ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 40 km
Explanation: Actual distance = Map distance × Scale factor = 7 cm × 5 km/cm = 35 kilometers.
Section 2: Integers & Expression Expressions

8. Sub-Zero Temperatures
At midnight, the temperature in a mountain village was -4°C. By morning, the temperature dropped by another 3°C. What was the temperature in the morning?
A) -1°C
B) -7°C ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 1°C
D) 7°C
Explanation: Start at -4°C and subtract 3°C (drop): -4 - 3 = -7°C.

9. Evaluating Algebraic Expressions
What is the value of the expression 3x + 5 when x = 4?
A) 12
B) 17 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 20
D) 27
Explanation: Substitute x = 4 into the expression: 3(4) + 5 = 12 + 5 = 17.

10. Order of Operations (PEMDAS)
What is the correct result for the calculation: 18 - 2 × 3 + 4?
A) 12
B) 16 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 52
D) 56
Explanation: Multiply first: 2 × 3 = 6. Then perform addition and subtraction from left to right: 18 - 6 + 4 = 12 + 4 = 16.

11. Exponent Practice
What is the value of 24 + 32?
A) 14
B) 22
C) 25 ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 32
Explanation: 24 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 16. 32 = 3 × 3 = 9. Adding them together: 16 + 9 = 25.

12. Ocean Depth Elevation
A submarine is floating at -150 meters relative to sea level. It rises by 60 meters. What is its new position?
A) -90 meters ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) -210 meters
C) 90 meters
D) 210 meters
Explanation: Starting position -150 m plus elevation +60 m: -150 + 60 = -90 meters.
Section 3: Geometry, Area & Volume

13. Area of a Triangle
A triangular park banner has a base of 10 meters and a height of 6 meters. What is its surface area?
A) 16 sq m
B) 30 sq m ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 60 sq m
D) 120 sq m
Explanation: Area of a triangle = (1/2) × base × height = (1/2) × 10 × 6 = 5 × 6 = 30 square meters.

14. Volume of a Fish Tank
A rectangular fish tank has a length of 50 cm, width of 20 cm, and height of 30 cm. What is the volume of the tank in cubic centimeters?
A) 3,000 cm³
B) 10,000 cm³
C) 30,000 cm³ ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 300,000 cm³
Explanation: Volume = length × width × height = 50 × 20 × 30 = 1,000 × 30 = 30,000 cm³.

15. Area of a Parallelogram
A paved pathway is shaped like a parallelogram with a base of 8 meters and a perpendicular height of 4 meters. What is its area?
A) 16 sq m
B) 24 sq m
C) 32 sq m ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 64 sq m
Explanation: Area of a parallelogram = base × height = 8 × 4 = 32 square meters.

16. Coordinate Grid Distance
Point A is located at (2, 3) and Point B is located at (2, 9) on a standard coordinate plane. What is the vertical distance between Points A and B?
A) 4 units
B) 6 units ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 8 units
D) 12 units
Explanation: Since x-coordinates are identical (2), find the difference in y-coordinates: 9 - 3 = 6 units.

17. Surface Area of a Cube
A wooden block cube measures 3 cm on each edge. What is the total surface area of all six faces of the cube?
A) 27 sq cm
B) 54 sq cm ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 36 sq cm
D) 72 sq cm
Explanation: Area of 1 face = 3 × 3 = 9 sq cm. A cube has 6 identical faces: 6 × 9 = 54 square centimeters.

18. Missing Angle in a Triangle
Two interior angles of a triangle measure 50° and 70°. What is the measure of the third interior angle?
A) 60° ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) 70°
C) 80°
D) 90°
Explanation: The sum of interior angles in any triangle is 180°. 180° - (50° + 70°) = 180° - 120° = 60°.
Section 4: Data Analysis, Probability & Equations

19. Solving Simple One-Step Equations
Solve for y: y + 14 = 35.
A) 19
B) 21 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 22
D) 49
Explanation: Subtract 14 from both sides: y = 35 - 14 = 21.

20. Finding the Median Score
Here are five test scores: 7, 12, 15, 18, 20. What is the median score of this dataset?
A) 12
B) 15 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 14.4
D) 18
Explanation: The data is already ordered from least to greatest. The middle (3rd) number is 15.

21. Range of Daily Temperatures
Over five days, recorded temperatures were: 18°C, 22°C, 15°C, 25°C, 20°C. What is the range of these temperatures?
A) 10°C ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) 12°C
C) 15°C
D) 25°C
Explanation: Range = Maximum value - Minimum value = 25°C - 15°C = 10°C.

22. Fair Spinner Probability
A spinner is divided into 8 equal sections numbered 1 through 8. What is the probability of spinning an even number?
A) 1/4
B) 1/2 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 3/8
D) 5/8
Explanation: Even numbers are 2, 4, 6, 8 (4 outcomes out of 8 possible). Probability = 4/8 = 1/2.

23. Reading a Double Bar Graph Concept
In a survey comparing preferred sports between Grade 5 and Grade 6, 16 Grade 6 students preferred soccer out of 40 total Grade 6 students. What percentage of Grade 6 students preferred soccer?
A) 30%
B) 35%
C) 40% ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 45%
Explanation: Percentage = (16 / 40) × 100 = 0.40 × 100 = 40%.

24. Multiplicative Equation Solving
Solve for m: 6m = 42.
A) 6
B) 7 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 8
D) 36
Explanation: Divide both sides by 6: m = 42 ÷ 6 = 7.

25. Calculating Mean Heights
Four plants have heights of 10 cm, 12 cm, 14 cm, and 16 cm. What is the mean height of the plants?
A) 12 cm
B) 13 cm ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 14 cm
D) 15 cm
Explanation: Mean = Sum of heights ÷ count = (10 + 12 + 14 + 16) ÷ 4 = 52 ÷ 4 = 13 cm.
