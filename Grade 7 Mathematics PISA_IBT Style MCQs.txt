Grade 7 Mathematics Challenge: Real-World Math Explorers
Welcome to the Grade 7 Math Challenge! Read each real-world context carefully, select the best option, and review the detailed explanation below each question.
Section 1: Ratios, Proportional Relationships & Percentages

1. School Store Markup
The school store buys notebooks for $2.00 each and sells them with a 30% markup. What is the selling price of one notebook?
A) $2.30
B) $2.60 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) $2.80
D) $3.00
Explanation: Markup amount = 30% of $2.00 = 0.30 × 2.00 = $0.60. Selling price = Original cost + Markup = $2.00 + $0.60 = $2.60.

2. Restaurant Tip Calculation
A family's dinner bill comes to $65.00. They decide to leave a 15% tip for the server. How much tip do they leave?
A) $9.75 ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) $10.00
C) $11.25
D) $13.00
Explanation: Tip = 15% of $65 = 0.15 × 65 = $9.75.

3. Constant of Proportionality
A baker uses 6 cups of flour to make 3 batches of cookies. Which equation models the proportional relationship between flour (f) and batches (b)?
A) f = 0.5b
B) f = 2b ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) f = 3b
D) f = b + 3
Explanation: Constant of proportionality (k) = Flour ÷ Batches = 6 ÷ 3 = 2. Thus, the equation is f = 2b.

4. Sales Tax in a Clothing Store
Maya buys a jacket priced at $50. If the sales tax rate is 8%, what is the total cost including tax?
A) $50.80
B) $53.00
C) $54.00 ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) $58.00
Explanation: Tax = 8% of $50 = 0.08 × 50 = $4.00. Total cost = $50 + $4 = $54.00.

5. Simple Interest Savings
Alex deposits $200 into a savings account earning 5% simple interest per year. How much total interest will he earn after 3 years?
A) $10
B) $20
C) $30 ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) $230
Explanation: Simple Interest I = P × r × t = $200 × 0.05 × 3 = $30.

6. Percentage Increase in Attendance
Last year, 40 students joined the chess club. This year, attendance grew to 50 students. What is the percentage increase in attendance?
A) 20%
B) 25% ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 30%
D) 80%
Explanation: Increase = 50 - 40 = 10. Percentage increase = (Increase ÷ Original) × 100 = (10 ÷ 40) × 100 = 0.25 × 100 = 25%.

7. Unit Rates with Complex Fractions
A worker paints 1/2 of a wall in 1/4 of an hour. At this rate, how many whole walls can the worker paint in 1 hour?
A) 1 wall
B) 2 walls ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 3 walls
D) 4 walls
Explanation: Unit rate = (1/2) ÷ (1/4) = (1/2) × (4/1) = 4/2 = 2 walls per hour.
Section 2: Rational Numbers & Expressions

8. Multiplying Rational Numbers
What is the value of (-3/4) × (-8/9)?
A) 2/3 ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) -2/3
C) 27/32
D) -27/32
Explanation: Multiplying two negative numbers gives a positive result: (3 × 8) / (4 × 9) = 24 / 36. Simplifying by dividing numerator and denominator by 12 gives 2/3.

9. Bank Account Transactions
Jordan's bank account starts at $150. He withdraws $45 on Monday and deposits $30 on Tuesday. What is his final account balance?
A) $75
B) $135 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) $165
D) $225
Explanation: Start: $150. Withdrawal (-$45): 150 - 45 = 105. Deposit (+$30): 105 + 30 = $135.

10. Distributive Property Expansion
Expand the algebraic expression: -4(2x - 5).
A) -8x - 20
B) -8x + 20 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) -8x - 5
D) 8x + 20
Explanation: Distribute -4 to both terms inside the parentheses: (-4 × 2x) + (-4 × -5) = -8x + 20.

11. Subtracting Linear Expressions
Simplify the expression: (5x + 7) - (2x + 3).
A) 3x + 4 ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) 3x + 10
C) 7x + 4
D) 7x + 10
Explanation: Distribute the negative sign: 5x + 7 - 2x - 3 = (5x - 2x) + (7 - 3) = 3x + 4.

12. Converting Fractions to Decimals
Which decimal is equivalent to the rational number 7/8?
A) 0.78
B) 0.85
C) 0.875 ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 0.88
Explanation: Divide 7 by 8: 7 ÷ 8 = 0.875.
Section 3: Geometry & Spatial Measurement

13. Area of a Circle
A circular garden pond has a radius of 7 meters. Using π ≈ 22/7, what is the approximate area of the pond?
A) 44 sq m
B) 154 sq m ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 308 sq m
D) 616 sq m
Explanation: Area = πr² = (22/7) × 7 × 7 = 22 × 7 = 154 square meters.

14. Circumference of a Bicycle Wheel
A bicycle wheel has a diameter of 70 cm. Using π ≈ 22/7, what is the circumference of the wheel?
A) 110 cm
B) 220 cm ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 440 cm
D) 1,540 cm
Explanation: Circumference C = πd = (22/7) × 70 = 22 × 10 = 220 cm.

15. Complementary Angles
Two angles are complementary. If one angle measures 38°, what is the measure of the second angle?
A) 52° ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) 62°
C) 142°
D) 152°
Explanation: Complementary angles add up to 90°. Second angle = 90° - 38° = 52°.

16. Supplementary Angles along a Line
Angle A and Angle B form a linear pair (supplementary angles). If Angle A measures 115°, what is the measure of Angle B?
A) 55°
B) 65° ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 75°
D) 245°
Explanation: Supplementary angles add up to 180°. Angle B = 180° - 115° = 65°.

17. Volume of a Triangular Prism
A triangular prism shaped tent has a triangular base with base b = 4 meters and height h = 3 meters. The length of the prism is 6 meters. What is its volume?
A) 18 cubic meters
B) 36 cubic meters ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 72 cubic meters
D) 108 cubic meters
Explanation: Base Area = (1/2) × b × h = (1/2) × 4 × 3 = 6 sq m. Volume = Base Area × Length = 6 × 6 = 36 cubic meters.

18. Scale Drawing Area Scale
A rectangular blueprint has dimensions of 4 cm by 5 cm. If the scale is 1 cm = 2 meters, what is the actual area of the room?
A) 20 sq m
B) 40 sq m
C) 80 sq m ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 160 sq m
Explanation: Actual dimensions: Length = 5 × 2 = 10 m, Width = 4 × 2 = 8 m. Actual Area = 10 × 8 = 80 square meters.
Section 4: Equations, Inequalities & Probability

19. Solving Two-Step Equations
Solve for x: 3x - 7 = 14.
A) x = 3
B) x = 7 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) x = 8
D) x = 21
Explanation: Add 7 to both sides: 3x = 21. Divide by 3: x = 7.

20. Solving Inequalities with Division
Solve the inequality: 2y + 5 > 13.
A) y > 3
B) y > 4 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) y < 4
D) y > 9
Explanation: Subtract 5 from both sides: 2y > 8. Divide by 2: y > 4.

21. Modeling Real-World Equations
A taxi ride costs a flat fee of $3.00 plus $2.00 per mile. If the total fare was $15.00, which equation represents the number of miles (m) driven?
A) 3m + 2 = 15
B) 2m + 3 = 15 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 5m = 15
D) 2m - 3 = 15
Explanation: Total Cost = (Cost per mile × miles) + Flat Fee → 15 = 2m + 3.

22. Compound Probability (Two Independent Events)
You flip a fair coin and roll a standard 6-sided die. What is the probability of flipping Heads AND rolling a 5?
A) 1/2
B) 1/6
C) 1/12 ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 2/3
Explanation: P(Heads) = 1/2. P(Rolling 5) = 1/6. Independent probability = (1/2) × (1/6) = 1/12.

23. Probability without Replacement
A bag contains 3 red balls and 2 blue balls. You pick one ball without looking, do NOT replace it, and pick a second ball. What is the probability that both balls picked are red?
A) 3/10 ✔ (Correct Answer)
B) 9/25
C) 2/5
D) 1/2
Explanation: P(1st Red) = 3/5. Remaining balls = 2 red out of 4 total. P(2nd Red) = 2/4. Total probability = (3/5) × (2/4) = 6/20 = 3/10.

24. Mean Absolute Deviation (MAD) Concept
A dataset has values: 10, 12, 14. The mean of this dataset is 12. What is the Mean Absolute Deviation (MAD)?
A) 0
B) 1.33 ✔ (Correct Answer)
C) 2
D) 4
Explanation: Absolute deviations from mean 12: |10 - 12| = 2, |12 - 12| = 0, |14 - 12| = 2. MAD = Sum of deviations ÷ Count = (2 + 0 + 2) ÷ 3 = 4/3 ≈ 1.33.

25. Random Sampling Inference
A school cafeteria manager surveys a random sample of 50 Grade 7 students and finds 30 prefer pizza. If there are 300 Grade 7 students in total, how many are estimated to prefer pizza?
A) 120 students
B) 150 students
C) 180 students ✔ (Correct Answer)
D) 200 students
Explanation: Proportion = 30 / 50 = 0.60 (60%). Estimated population total = 60% of 300 = 0.60 × 300 = 180 students.
